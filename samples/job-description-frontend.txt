Senior Frontend Engineer
Northwind Analytics — Remote (US/EU time zones) — Full-time

About us
Northwind Analytics builds a real-time business intelligence platform used by 4,000+ companies to explore their data. Our product team is small, senior and ships weekly.

The role
We are looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to own key parts of our dashboard experience: interactive charts, a drag-and-drop report builder, and a component library shared across three product teams. You will work closely with design and backend engineers and help raise the bar on performance, accessibility and testing.

What you will do
- Build and maintain complex, data-heavy UIs in React and TypeScript
- Own our design system (Storybook) and drive adoption across teams
- Improve performance: bundle size, rendering of large tables and charts, Core Web Vitals
- Design frontend architecture and state management for real-time features (WebSockets)
- Write unit, integration and end-to-end tests (Vitest, Testing Library, Playwright)
- Mentor mid-level engineers through code review and pairing
- Partner with product and design to scope features and make trade-offs

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional frontend development experience
- Expert knowledge of React, TypeScript and modern CSS (Tailwind or CSS-in-JS)
- Experience building or maintaining a component library / design system
- Strong understanding of web performance and accessibility (WCAG 2.1)
- Experience with data visualization libraries (D3, Recharts, ECharts or similar)
- Comfortable with REST and GraphQL APIs
- Clear written communication; experience working in a remote team

Nice to have
- Next.js or server-side rendering experience
- Node.js backend experience
- CI/CD with GitHub Actions; Docker
- Experience in a B2B SaaS or analytics product

What we offer
- Salary range: $150,000 – $185,000 + equity
- Fully remote, flexible hours, 4 weeks of vacation
- $2,000 annual learning budget
